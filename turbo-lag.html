Turbo lag is not the problem that you think it is.

Whatever turbo you get is going to have a boost threshold - a minimum RPM required to make full boost.

If you're well above that threshold, lag is imperceptible even with an older large-ish turbo. It's been a solved problem for a long time.

If you're well below that threshold, you're not going to make good boost until you get above it - but that's not lag, that's just being in the wrong gear.

Downshift a gear or two - whatever it takes to get RPM above the threshold - and then stomp the throttle. Then you not only get all of the power from your bigger turbo, you also get the mechanical advantage of the lower gear. It's win-win.

If course you can over-do it. If your rev limit is 7000 and your turbo doesn't make full boost until 5000, you're going to be shifting gears a lot just to stay in the narrow 2000 RPM window. Even with a 4000 RPM threshold I felt the powerband was a bit narrow, with a 7000 RPM rev limit, but now with a 4,000-8,000 window it's perfect.

(This also applies to camshafts.)
